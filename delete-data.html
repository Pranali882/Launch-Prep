Delete your EstelMiti data

Your entries

  • Your entries, notes and settings are stored only on your own device. We keep no copy and cannot see them.
  • To remove them, open More > Clear all data in the app, or uninstall the app (on the web, clear the site's data).

Your account (only if you signed in to use a paid plan)

  • Signing in is optional. If you did, we keep a small account record: your sign-in ID, your email address (or Apple's private relay address), and your plan and its end date.
  • In the app: open More > Account & plan > Delete account and confirm. This removes your sign-in and the plan record.
  • If you cannot use the app: email estelmiti882@gmail.com from the address you signed in with and ask for your account to be deleted.
  • Deleting your account does not cancel a subscription: cancel it in Google Play or the App Store.

Feedback you sent

  • Feedback is anonymous. If you quote your feedback reference number (EM-XXXXXX) in an email to estelmiti882@gmail.com, that message will be deleted.
