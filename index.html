EstelMiti Privacy Policy

Effective date: 28 October 2026
Developer: Pranali Sridhar Daram, an independent developer
Privacy contact and grievance officer: Pranali, estelmiti882@gmail.com

EstelMiti is a personal finance calculator and tracker for Android, iPhone and the web, built on one idea: your financial data belongs on your device, or in your own cloud backup, not on our servers. This policy describes only what the app actually does.

At a glance

  • Your entries never leave your device. Accounts, investments, loans, goals, amounts and notes are stored on your phone (on the web, in your browser). We have no server that holds them and cannot see a single one.
  • Your data is never used for advice, marketing or partners. No ads, no promotions, no cross-selling. We do not sell, rent, share or trade data, build profiles, or pass anything to advertisers, data brokers, banks, brokers, insurers or other financial companies. We do not scrape or combine data about you from other apps or websites, or track you across them.
  • Only three things can reach us, each set out below: feedback you choose to send, crash reports, and, if you sign in for a paid plan, a sign-in and plan record.

On your device

  • Everything you enter stays with you: entries, holdings, goals, notes, files you import (CSV or Excel), your PIN or fingerprint / Face ID lock, your preferences and the note that you accepted the disclaimer.
  • Backups and reports you export (backup file, PDF, CSV) are created on your device and go only where you choose to save or share them.
  • EstelMiti keeps its data out of your phone's automatic cloud backups.
  • You are in full control: edit or delete any entry, use Clear all data, or uninstall the app (on the web, clear the site's data).
  • We never hold a copy, so we cannot recover lost data. Keep your own backups.

What the app reads

  • The app reads a little public information from our data server (hosted by Supabase), such as daily exchange rates for major currencies against the Indian rupee, publicly published rates (such as deposit or savings scheme rates) and a few on/off settings that apply to every user.
  • Values like these are suggestions you can change. Nothing about you or your entries is sent to get them, and the app works fully with values you type yourself.
  • Like any web server, ours briefly logs technical details of each connection (such as IP address and time) to stay working and secure. Our hosting provider keeps these for a few days, and they are not linked to you.
  • The app does not fetch stock or fund prices. It may show links to official or other public sources (such as AMFI, NSE, BSE, RBI Retail Direct or NPS Trust) as suggestions only.
  • Whether a link opens depends on your location, network and the site's availability; it opens in your own browser, under that site's own terms and policy. Whether you use a link, a suggested value or your own figure is always your decision.

What can reach us

Feedback

  • When: only when you tap Send feedback
  • What it contains: the text you type and a random reference number
  • Why: to read it and improve the app
  • Kept for: up to 12 months

Crash reports

  • When: only if the app crashes
  • What it contains: error type, where in the app's code it happened, phone model, system and app version, a random install ID
  • Why: to find and fix bugs
  • Kept for: about 90 days

Sign-in and plan record

  • When: only if you sign in (paid plans)
  • What it contains: sign-in ID, email (or Apple's private relay address), plan, end date
  • Why: to make your plan work on your devices
  • Kept for: until you delete your account
  • Crash reports never contain your entries, amounts, names or notes, and are not linked to your sign-in.
  • Feedback reaches our inbox through a form service; please keep personal or financial details out of your message.

Sign-in and paid plans

  • Signing in is optional and never needed during the free trial. It is only needed to use a paid plan on another device or on the web.
  • You sign in with Google or Apple, and we never see your password.
  • The record shows only that you have a plan; your financial entries are never part of it, and we cannot see them.
  • Payments, subscriptions, renewals, cancellations and refunds are handled entirely by Google Play or the Apple App Store, under their terms. We never see your card or bank details.
  • To delete your account, go to More > Account & plan > Delete account, or email estelmiti882@gmail.com from the address you signed in with. This removes your sign-in and plan record. It does not cancel a subscription; do that in Google Play or the App Store.

Who helps run the app

A few providers run the features above, on our behalf and under their own privacy policies. Their servers may be outside your country, including in the United States.

  • Supabase: public data server
  • Web3Forms: delivers feedback to our inbox
  • Google Firebase: crash reports, sign-in and plan records
  • Google Play and Apple App Store: downloads, purchases and subscriptions. Like every app store, they also see standard details such as installs and device type, under their own privacy policies; EstelMiti does not add to or control this.

Security and permissions

  • Everything the app sends or receives travels over an encrypted connection (HTTPS), and each sign-in record can be read only by its own user.
  • Your app lock is checked by your device and never sent to us.
  • On Android the app blocks screenshots; on iPhone it hides the screen in the app switcher.
  • It also warns you if the phone appears rooted or jailbroken, a check that runs on your device and sends nothing.
  • No system is completely secure, so please use a screen lock and keep backups safe.

The app asks only for:

  • Internet (for the uses above)
  • Fingerprint / Face ID (only if you turn on app lock)
  • Files (only when you back up, restore, import or export)

Your choices

  • Your entries are always under your control, as described above.
  • Feedback and sign-in are optional, and you can delete your sign-in record in the app at any time.
  • You may also have rights under the law where you live; privacy requests can be sent to the contact below.

Children

EstelMiti is meant for adults (18 and over) and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly receive information from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, for example because a feature is added, we will update this page and its effective date before that version of the app is released.
